Faculty Publication Analyzer

Type a professor's name to see what they've published in recent years: output, citations, authorship role, research themes, collaborators and journals. Every paper links out to Google Scholar.

Citations, topics and h-index come from OpenAlex, which needs a free API key (create an account, then copy it from openalex.org/settings/api). Without a key the site still works from PubMed alone, just without citation counts. Settings stay in this browser only.

Author profiles matched
Common names can have several profiles, and one person is sometimes split across two. Tick every profile that is this professor, then re-run.

    Tap a name to open the full analysis. Profiles are picked automatically here, so check the full view for common names.
    Data: OpenAlex (works, citations, topics, h-index) and PubMed (NCBI E-utilities), queried live from your browser. Google Scholar has no public API, so it is linked rather than queried; its citation counts usually run 10–40% higher than OpenAlex. Figures are for screening, not formal evaluation.
